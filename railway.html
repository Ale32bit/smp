Standards

1. Railway placement

Railways must be built above the surface of the world around the Y60 range, tunnels can be built to pass through mountains granted that there is no station to connect to.

Water bodies (such as rivers, lakes, oceans) must be avoided if possible except in certain cases:

  • The railway has to go over a river, but not along. In this case a bridge of sufficient height must be built and must not close the river.
  • The railway has to pass through an ocean to connect another land. In this case a tunnel must be built below the sea surface of the ocean.

Other exceptions can be agreed for certain cases.

A bed for the railways must be built if above the surface and must be illuminated to prevent the spawn of mobs.

2. Tunnels

Tunnel walls must not present holes and must be illuminated to prevent the spawn of mobs. Tunnels must be big enough to fit the standard size of the train, plus one or more blocks. It is advised to dig a tunnel dedicated to walking and must be accessible from the railway.
